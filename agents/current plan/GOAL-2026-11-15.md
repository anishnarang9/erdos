## Acceptance: what counts as done

1. The buyer approves a fresh problem, its exact dataset, normalized rules, required files, provider, price, deadline, and independent oracle. The approved job must use the supported general-purpose rule catalog. An unsupported requirement must fail before funding.
2. The API compiles the approved revision with real engine provenance. Confirmed Arc job terms bind chain, core, job ID, provider, checker image, rules, references, budget, and expiry.
3. An authorized buyer creates and funds exactly one primary job for `1_000_000` USDC base units. A fresh proof for that job passes local checks and a real Arc submission emits `ProofAccepted`.
4. A separate release succeeds on Arc. It emits the matching key and `PaymentReleased` for the exact bounty. A buyer independently retrieves ciphertext and the key from confirmed chain data, recovers the same files through SDK and engine, and passes the independent oracle.
5. Negative paths and restart guards have clearly labelled local or read-only evidence. Any extra **live** rejection or refund transaction needs its own exact authorization.
6. The final candidate passes required checks. A normal PR merge places the tested tree and sanitized evidence on remote `main`. A separate clean checkout reproduces the documented checks and read-only evidence verification.

**Not sufficient:** a synthetic funded job, a forkless local chain with Arc's chain ID, a proof for a different job, a passing CI badge, or merged code alone. Do not claim a buyer UI, live Grok call for this new workload, or production custody security.
